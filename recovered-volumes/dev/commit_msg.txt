Retrieval v1 gate: minimax query planner, role-anchored templates, eval parity

Trace gate at 2000 held-out episodes (seed 20260824, contradiction 0.15,
filler 0.25): all_hops_top1 0.9803, drop_rate 0.0187. Per-domain drop:
corporate 0.0085, kinship_temporal 0.0004, logistics 0.0746,
regulatory 0.0000.

- src/train/retrieval.py: when the greedy margin query fails, a bounded
  minimax pass picks integer repeat counts for the candidate terms. BM25
  scoring is linear in a query term counts vector, so query choice is a
  small game against the strongest rival document; multiplicative weights
  approximate it and every rounding is verified against the real
  retriever. Queries still draw only on question terms plus chunks from
  earlier hops.
- src/worldgen/domains.py: document templates restate the entity on the
  side of each relation that questions anchor on. Bag-of-words retrieval
  cannot see argument roles, so the restatement puts the role into term
  frequency. This changes the rendered corpus; gate metrics and the
  held-out JSONL were regenerated after the change.
- src/evals/interactive.py: the decode loop now serves retrieval under
  the training oracle contracts, with reliability-weighted ranking and
  without-replacement serving across one call rounds.
- PREP.md: final v1 metrics, pinned render settings, render status, and
  the remaining kill-test work.

Also lands the pending working-tree work: retrieval v1 multi-hop traces,
trace_stats and debug_drops diagnostics, and the interactive eval loop
with its tests.
