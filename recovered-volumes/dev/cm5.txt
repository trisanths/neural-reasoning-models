src/norm: what the 0.65 between the parser and the 167M reader is made of

On the eight shapes the key-position axis reaches, xxl167 reads 0.3938 exact
key-first on 2,016 items and 0.3392 value-first on 1,984, against the parser at
2,016 of 2,016 and 1,984 of 1,984. The gap had never been decomposed.

The committed records cannot decompose it. results/system/eval/xxl167/ carries
four outcome flags, the executed answer and the gold answer, and not the
emitted structure, so malformed, refused and exact are recoverable from it and
wrong kind, wrong binding and wrong content are one undifferentiated flag. The
structures were emitted again from the same checkpoint through the same
neval.emit at the same settings, and the pass is gated on reproducing the
committed record item by item on exact, malformed, refused, wrong and answer
before any count is taken. The gate reads 7,000 of 7,000 for both decodes of
xxl167 and for l45 and xl93, and the recovered exact rates reproduce
THRESHOLD.md to the digit.

The decomposition. Of 1,222 key-first failures: malformed 0.0090, refused
0.0933, wrong kind 0.1047, wrong binding 0.4804, wrong content 0.3126. Of 1,311
value-first failures: 0.0031, 0.1098, 0.1327, 0.4638, 0.2906. Right structure
with a wrong answer downstream is 0 of 2,016 and 0 of 1,984, empty by
construction and counted anyway. Sampled decoding reproduces every cell within
0.003.

The gap is spread. No shape is above 0.6310 exact and none is closer than 0.37
to the parser; shape contributions to the failure count run 0.2013 to 0.0761.
Bringing the three shapes whose failure is a clean transposition to 1.0000
recovers 319 of 1,222 key-first failures and leaves the figure at 0.5521.

The dominant mode is binding, and it is the structural one. Malformed output is
under one percent of failures and wrong kind is a tenth, of which 123 of 128
key-first and 171 of 174 value-first are a transposed table with a compensating
invert-for-lookup that returns gold's answer, so they are binding failures
wearing a different plan operation. Counting those with the binding bucket puts
the mode at about 0.58 and 0.59 of the gap. The whole-table transposition rate
does not fall with capacity, it spreads: 0 of 514 key-first failures at 45M and
3 of 607 at 93M against 324 of 1,222 at 167M, while the value-first rate holds
near 0.39 at all three. Scale stopped the reader applying one convention
everywhere; it did not teach it which word is the key.

The remainder is content corruption in the shapes with more definitions, more
entries and longer plans, and that half is the ordinary kind of failure more
capacity or data plausibly moves. A 7B-token 350M pretrain would be testing the
smaller half of the gap.

Two shape-specific findings are worth carrying. exclusion fails by misplacing
one or two rows of a four-row table, which odd_one_out's precondition then
refuses, so its 0.4073 value-first refusal rate is the design failing safe on a
precision error. And answers run well above structures, 0.5258 and 0.5076
against 0.3938 and 0.3392, because a wrong structure executes to gold's answer
on about a quarter of failures.

Every record file, summary and interval is under results/norm/gap/ and named in
the document.
