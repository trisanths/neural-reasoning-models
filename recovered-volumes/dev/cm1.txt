src/extern: the verdict is checked against the records before it is spliced

results/extern/verdict.md is hand written and pasted verbatim into LIQUID.md on
every build, so a retracted claim in it propagates instead of decaying. The
retrieval paragraph said the reader "almost never emits a retrieval request",
with near zero Exa spend as the evidence. The n=400 record has the reader
emitting the token on 102 of 400 items, 0.2550 [0.215, 0.300], with 73 live
searches beside 255 cache hits, and a cache hit costs nothing, so spend was
never a rate. The paragraph now reports the rate, and the finding it carries is
that asking buys nothing: 0.0100 with a live index and 0.0100 without, and on
the 88 items where a chunk entered the trace the reader named an option zero
times either way.

Two more claims did not survive a recount. The per family split said below
chance on three families and above on one; the artifact has two above and two
below, one of them by a third of a point on 25 items. And the general cell's
0.0000 now carries its states: of 26 items, 7 ran, 17 were malformed and 2 were
refused, so the losing arm rests on 7 scored answers.

The mechanism is `check_verdict` in `src/extern/md.py`. The verdict carries a
claim block binding every numeral in its prose to a record file and a field,
and the build raises when a stated value is not what the file holds, when a
numeral appears in the prose that no line accounts for, when a claimed value
has dropped out of the prose, or when a cited record file is newer than the
verdict quoting it. A missing verdict file is an error rather than a silent
"(verdict pending)". Four negative tests, one per branch, were run and each
raised.
