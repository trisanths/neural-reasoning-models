src/pilot: the acquisition pilot, sibling and blank controls, and length-normalised forced choice

bench.py gains two controls beside the oracle. Sibling fills the oracle's
chapter slots from a structurally matched rival system (same names and
glyphs, other definitions); blank keeps the slots and empties them. The
eight guard checks run a second time against what each control serves
(Guard.check_served), with ablation restated as "the sibling's own answer
differs from the gold". refuniverse and the algebra adapter implement
sibling() and reference_answer(); refuniverse gains int_answers so every
level answers one number below its modulus.

mc.py scored options by summed NLL, which favours short options, and broke
ties toward index 0. It now scores mean NLL per option token, in a seeded
shuffled order, with seeded tie breaks. legacy=True restores the old rule;
nothing passes it. heldout.py seeds each question separately.

src/pilot holds the rest: the item generator (refuniverse, algebra, and the
binary_op and simple rule families, seeds checked against the training
manifests), the strict grader with its hedging canary, PopQA as a four-way
forced choice, the vLLM adapter (base completion, chat with thinking off,
thinking capped at 4,096 tokens), the resume-safe runner mirrored to S3, and
the report that applies the preregistered decision and refuses stale
records. scripts/pilot_*.py are thin entry points.
