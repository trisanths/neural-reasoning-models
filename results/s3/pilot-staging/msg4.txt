src/pilot/PILOT.md: how to run the pilot, the smoke run, and what the full run costs

The smoke run (LFM2.5-350M-Base and Qwen3-0.6B with thinking off and on, 24
items in four conditions, 200 PopQA items) went through items, vLLM,
grading, records, the S3 mirror and the report without a failure. A
throughput probe on 384 prompts from the full item set puts the full run at
about 10 GPU-hours on the L40S, 96% of it in the four thinking-on
configurations. vLLM's PopQA likelihoods match a transformers forward pass
to bfloat16 noise.
