Latent-space transfer between models for efficient reasoning

Architecture
Method
The large model is 67× the size of the small one. Both learn to reason in vectors on their own first, then are frozen. Only the two projectors (two-layer MLP with GELU and RMS scale matching, 0.08M parameters together) and the rank-8 adapter are trained for the handoff, following the residual-stream maps of [4].

Models were tested on 2000 held-out multi-step arithmetic chains like start 3, add 4, times 2, add 1, answered mod 10. The handoff matches the large model at 0.76× its compute. On 1400-token contexts, compressing the message is 12× cheaper than running the large model with a written chain of thought.
Scaling, in progress. The result above is at 0.12M → 8.0M. I am walking it up one step at a time: a few hundred million → 1B, then 0.5B → 7B, then 0.5B → 32B. Each step is pretty different, since the two models have to agree on a shared latent format and that gets harder as they diverge further apart in size and capability.
